## Page 1: Laser flange forceps

**title:** Laser flange forceps

**subtitle:** Forceps for laser-assisted flange creation in Yamane fixation

**role:** Surgical instrument | Role: Inventor; design and prototyping | Status: Under development

**problem:**

- Our endolaser technique creates a precise flange in Yamane scleral fixation
- Surgeon feedback: aligning the laser tip with the haptic by hand is difficult

**solutions:**

- Forceps hold the laser tip and the haptic side by side
- Alignment becomes stable and repeatable

## Page 2: Automatic VA kiosk

**title:** Automatic VA kiosk

**subtitle:** Voice-guided, self-service visual acuity test

**role:** Medical device prototype | Role: Concept, test algorithm, interaction design and prototype | Status: Adapting software for production; feasibility testing

**problem:**

- Every eye patient needs a visual acuity test, and each test occupies one staff member
- At busy clinics this adds to waiting time

**solutions:**

- Spoken instructions guide the patient through the test
- On-device speech recognition grades each answer
- The result prints automatically, freeing staff for other care

**media 1 caption:** Animation redrawn from the kiosk software: the patient stands at 6 m and reads each line aloud; the kiosk grades the answers and prints the result. Illustrative values.

## Page 3: Eye drop compliance tracker

**title:** Eye drop compliance tracker

**subtitle:** Bottle-mounted camera and motion sensor that confirm the drop reached the eye

**role:** Research data logger; bench prototype | Role: Concept and device design | Status: On permanent hold (US patent barrier)

**problem:**

- Smart bottles count attempts (motion, squeeze, cap opening), not drops that reach the eye
- A drop that lands on the lid or cheek is still recorded as taken

**solutions:**

- A motion sensor wakes an infrared camera when the bottle is inverted
- Each instillation is filmed, time-stamped and stored on the device
- Planned: an on-device model trained on the clips, validated against fluorescein

**media 1 caption:** To-scale 3D model of the bench prototype. Drag to turn; tap a part to explore it.

## Page 5: Electronic CRF with automatic error detection

**title:** Electronic CRF with automatic error detection

**subtitle:** Case report form that checks each value as it is entered

**role:** Web application | Role: Application design to protocol and IRB requirements | Status: Pending mock data test

**problem:**

- Paper and spreadsheet forms accept any value
- Errors and missing fields surface weeks later, after the participant has left

**solutions:**

- Each value is validated as it is entered
- Out-of-range and missing values are flagged before the visit ends
- Optometrist, assistant and principal investigator work on linked screens

**media 1 caption:** Measuring a defocus curve on three linked screens: a mistyped value is flagged at once and re-measured. Illustrative values.

## Page 6: Globe tracker

**title:** Globe tracker

**subtitle:** Measures eye movement during cataract surgery from the surgical video

**role:** Video analysis software | Role: Concept and algorithm development | Status: Under development

**problem:**

- Eye movement during cataract surgery is not routinely measured
- Without a measurement, it is hard to teach or compare

**solutions:**

- Fits the limbus on every frame of a standard surgical video
- Reports globe pitch, yaw and torsion in degrees
- A first step towards objective feedback, training and robotic-surgery metrics

**media 1 caption:** Limbus fitted on the surgical view (left); globe pitch, yaw and torsion on a 3D model of the eye (right).
